--- PAGE 1: ACCURACY, CALIBRATION, AND DIAL SAFETY ---

IS 3624 specifies requirements for industrial dial-type pressure, vacuum, and compound gauges using Bourdon tubes, diaphragms, or bellows as sensing elements. The standard dial sizes range from 40 mm to 250 mm in diameter.

Accuracy classes are defined as Class 0.25 (master calibration gauges), Class 1.0 (standard process gauges), and Class 2.5/4.0 (field indicators). For refinery service, gauges must be Class 1.0, equipped with a laminated safety glass window and a blowout disc at the back of the case to relieve pressure in case of Bourdon tube rupture.
