--- PAGE 1: REFINING OVERVIEW AND INDIAN PETROLEUM SECTOR ---

NPTEL chemical engineering lectures cover crude oil composition and refining technologies. Crudes are analyzed using True Boiling Point (TBP) distillation curves to determine yield potential. Indian refineries process a mix of sweet and sour crudes from domestic fields (Bombay High) and imports.

Key thermal processes include Delayed Coking, which processes vacuum residue into petcoke, gas, and coker distillates, and Visbreaking, which reduces the viscosity of heavy residue to produce fuel oil.
