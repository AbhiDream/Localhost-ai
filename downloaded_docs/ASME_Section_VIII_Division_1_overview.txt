--- PAGE 1: PRESSURE VESSEL DESIGN RULES ---

ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code (BPVC) Section VIII Division 1 provides the design, fabrication, inspection, and testing rules for pressure vessels operating at pressures exceeding 15 psi (1 bar). It is the most widely adopted pressure vessel code globally.

Design rules cover cylindrical shells, spherical shells, and various head types including ellipsoidal (2:1), torispherical, and hemispherical heads. Allowable stress values are listed in ASME Section II Part D, which incorporates a safety factor of 3.5 on tensile strength.
