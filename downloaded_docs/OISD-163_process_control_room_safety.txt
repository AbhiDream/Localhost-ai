================================================================================
DOCUMENT: Oisd-163 Process Control Room Safety
================================================================================

--- PAGE 1: ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN AND BLAST RESISTANCE ---

OISD-STD-163 outlines safety requirements for the design, construction, and safety systems of process control rooms. The control room must be designed to withstand a potential vapor cloud explosion (VCE) with a blast overpressure of 0.3 bar for a duration of 100 milliseconds.

The building must be constructed of reinforced concrete, without windows on the sides facing process units. Doors must be heavy-duty blast doors equipped with self-closing mechanisms. Cable entry transits (cellars) must be sealed using certified fire-stop and gas-tight sealing modules.

--- PAGE 2: POSITIVE PRESSURE VENTILATION AND HVAC CONTROLS ---

To prevent the entry of toxic or flammable hydrocarbon vapors, the control room must be kept under a positive pressure of at least 5 mm water gauge relative to the outside atmosphere. The HVAC system must draw outdoor air from a stack located at least 15 meters above ground level, far from process units.

Hydrocarbon and toxic gas detectors (H2S, SO2) must be installed in the HVAC intake duct. If gas is detected at 10% LEL or 5 ppm H2S, the system must automatically switch to 100% recirculation mode and close the fresh air intake dampers. Emergency air breathing systems must be available inside the control room for operators.
